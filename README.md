# Studio 7 early prototypes

Early digital prototypes Studio 7 worked on during the Concept Phase.

Most prototypes are single HTML files that run in a browser. The live links below open them directly. You can also download the repo and open any `.html` file in your browser.

| Prototype | What it is | Try it |
|---|---|---|
| Spindelpaal 3D | A pole of ball screws that makes spheres breathe | [Open](https://emergence-delft.github.io/studio-7-early-prototypes/spindelpaal-3d.html) |
| Light-tree lamp | Sliding tube units that fold open into flowers of light | [Open](https://emergence-delft.github.io/studio-7-early-prototypes/light-tree-lamp/Light-tree%20lamp%20prototype.html) |
| Arrow Swarm | A swarm that comes when called and flees when scared | [Open](https://emergence-delft.github.io/studio-7-early-prototypes/arrow-swarm/Arrow%20Swarm.html) |
| Facing Life | Your face as a pixel grid, brought to life by the Game of Life | [Open](https://emergence-delft.github.io/studio-7-early-prototypes/facing-life/hex_mirror.html) |
| Video to Game of Life | Your webcam as black-and-white pixels; every white pixel becomes a living cell | [Open](https://emergence-delft.github.io/studio-7-early-prototypes/facing-life/video_to_game_of_life.html) |
| Game of Life – 5 colours | Your webcam in 5 colours, each running its own Game of Life | [Open](https://emergence-delft.github.io/studio-7-early-prototypes/facing-life/game_of_life_5_colours.html) |
| Pixel Life – network | Game of Life clusters growing a network of glowing nodes and lines | [Open](https://emergence-delft.github.io/studio-7-early-prototypes/facing-life/pixel_life_network.html) |
| Pixel Life 3D – 5 colours | 5 Games of Life stacked into a 3D tower, where height is time | [Open](https://emergence-delft.github.io/studio-7-early-prototypes/facing-life/pixel_life_3d_colors.html) |
